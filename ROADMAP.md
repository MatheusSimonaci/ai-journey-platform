**Status:** Board Approved
**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Supersedes:** v1.0 (Freemium SaaS model)

---

## Strategic Direction

The monetization model has pivoted from SaaS subscription to **infoproducts**. The bet:

1. Build audience first — no monetization before audience exists
2. Monetize via courses or cohort programs once audience is established
3. "Base de Conhecimento" is the anchor asset — a content hub that enables repurposing across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Instagram simultaneously
4. SaaS / industry-specific pivot remains on the horizon (month 3+) but is not the current bet

**Why infoproducts:** Our target users are Brazilian learners without deep technical backgrounds. Trust and content-based authority precede willingness to pay. Courses and cohorts match how this audience already buys online education. A subscription product requires brand recognition we don't have yet.

## 3. 60-Day Execution Roadmap

### Phase 1: Base de Conhecimento (Days 1–20)
*Goal: Build the content hub that becomes both a product feature and an audience-building tool.*
- Design and implement "Base de Conhecimento" — a structured knowledge repository linked to AI journey tracks
- Enable content export / repurposing format (LinkedIn post, tweet thread, Instagram caption) from the same source material
- KPI focus: Content created, platform reach (follower growth across channels)

### Phase 2: Domain Tracks + Cohort Foundation (Days 21–40)
*Goal: Deepen content value and lay the technical ground for cohort products.*
- Launch 3 domain-specific tracks (AI for Legal, AI for Creative, AI for Tech Management)
- Build basic cohort tooling: enrollment, cohort timeline view, group progress tracking
- KPI focus: Track completion rates, cohort sign-up waitlist size

### Phase 3: Community & First Cohort Prep (Days 41–60)
*Goal: Launch a freemium community layer and prepare first paid cohort offer.*
- Community discussions (freemium — read + post)
- "Trilha da Jornada de IA" cohort structure: structured 4-week program, group accountability
- First cohort landing page and waitlist
- KPI focus: Community DAU, waitlist size, first cohort pricing validation
